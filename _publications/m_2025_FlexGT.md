Abstract: This paper studies distributed stochastic optimization under non-IID data and the trade-off between communication and computation. We propose FlexGT, a flexible snapshot gradient tracking method with tunable local updates and neighbor communications per round. We derive tight communication and computational complexity bounds and introduce Acc-FlexGT, which achieves Pareto-optimal trade-offs up to logarithmic factors with prior knowledge of the graph. Numerical experiments corroborate the theoretical results and demonstrate the effectiveness of the proposed methods.

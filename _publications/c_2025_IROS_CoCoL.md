Abstract: Collaborative learning enhances the performance and adaptability of multi-robot systems but faces high communication overhead and data heterogeneity. We propose CoCoL, a communication-efficient decentralized collaborative learning method that combines mirror descent, approximate Newton-type updates, inexact subproblem solutions, and gradient tracking. Experiments on three representative multi-robot learning tasks show that CoCoL substantially reduces communication rounds and bandwidth consumption while maintaining state-of-the-art accuracy under non-IID data, streaming data, and time-varying network topologies.

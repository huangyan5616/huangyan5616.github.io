Abstract: This paper studies distributed minimax optimization over networks. We propose a distributed optimistic gradient tracking method, termed DOGT, which uses a surrogate function capturing the similarity between local objective functions to approximate a centralized optimistic approach locally. For strongly convex-strongly concave objectives, DOGT achieves linear convergence while remaining robust to heterogeneity. With accelerated consensus, ADOGT attains an optimal convergence rate and communication complexity. Numerical experiments demonstrate the effectiveness of the proposed methods.
